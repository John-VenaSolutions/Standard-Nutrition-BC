1What Vena would do for you

Vena is a budgeting, reporting, and analysis platform that keeps the Excel experience your team already knows, but replaces the broken links, static files, and manual follow-up with one controlled model. It pulls your actuals and driver data into one place, gives your budget owners a cleaner way to work, and helps you get to reports, drilldown, and scenario answers much faster.

What is getting in the way today

  • The feed-side budget cycle still takes about two months and runs through linked spreadsheets and manual checks.
  • Expense budgeting is still one of the most manual parts of the process.
  • Some operating-company questions can still take up to two weeks to answer accurately.
  • You said accuracy is the key approval metric, and the current process makes that harder than it should be.

What changes with Vena

  • Your 15 core budget owners work in one system instead of across fragile linked files.
  • Finance gets one place to manage budgeting, reporting, drilldown, and supporting detail.
  • You can compare budget to actual faster and get to the story behind the number more quickly.
  • Scenario work around hiring, regional growth, margin, and spending becomes easier to turn around.

One relevant example

You shared a case where one region was budgeted to deliver about $0.90 to the bottom line per point, but actual results came in closer to $0.35 per point. You could point to a few likely reasons, but you could not show the full story cleanly in the data. That is exactly the gap this case is trying to close.

If nothing changes

The likely outcome is another budget cycle built on the same linked files, the same manual follow-up, and the same slow turnaround when operating-company leaders ask what changed, where margin moved, or what a hiring or expansion decision would mean. That keeps finance reacting after the fact when the business really needs faster, clearer answers.
